Questions

Everything else you'd ask

How long does the 90-day free program last?

The program runs for 90 days from launch. Sellers pay zero commission on all orders during this period, and referral bonuses apply throughout.

When do the ₦300 bonuses actually pay out?

Both bonuses trigger automatically the moment a referred new customer completes their first order — ₦300 to the seller, ₦300 credit to the customer. No forms, no waiting.

What happens after the 90 days?

We announce simple, transparent pricing well before the program ends — and early sellers get loyalty terms. Nothing changes without telling you first.

Do customers need the app to order?

No. Buyers can order in-app or through a seller's referral link on any phone. Sellers can also create walk-in orders directly in Seller Studio.

How do I join as a seller?

Tap "Join as a Seller", complete the quick onboarding form, and you're listed. Our team verifies every seller to keep buyers safe.

Is it safe to pay through OGas?

Yes. Payments are processed through licensed payment providers, every order carries a unique verification code, and your receipt records the exact kg weighed.
